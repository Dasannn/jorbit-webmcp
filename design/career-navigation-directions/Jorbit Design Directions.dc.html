DIRECTION A
You are the centre of gravity
Metaphor. A gravity well. Distance from centre is effort-to-reach; the bright ring is the boundary of what you can reach today.
Layout. Full-bleed canvas, identity rail left, detail rail right, constraints as a bottom bar so the map never loses centre.
Opportunities. Nodes; radius = effort, size = volume of similar roles, label detail appears only inside the boundary. Faint spokes = your strongest evidence paths.
Blocked. Hollow dashed rings outside the boundary, unlabelled until you probe them — visibly out of orbit, never greyed-out list items.
Unlock. Amber. The boundary re-inflates, the old boundary lingers as a dashed ghost, unlocked bodies travel inward along a dashed trail and the counter rolls 31 → 54.
Simulation vs real. Ghost boundary + "SIMULATION ACTIVE" + explicit Keep/Revert. Real state is solid; simulated state is amber and reversible.
Agent. One pill in the top bar: a slow pulse and a count of what it re-ranked. Tappable for a trace; never a panel.
Strengths. Highest first-impression payoff; the cause-and-effect moment is unmistakable; scales to a hero animation for the demo.
Risks. Node density gets crowded past ~40 bodies; labels need collision logic; the metaphor must not hide a real list view.

DIRECTION B
Scan the market, read the clusters
Metaphor. An instrument. A slow sweep discovers bodies; bodies that share skills wire themselves into constellations you can name.
Layout. Instrument-in-a-frame: sector readout left, scope centre, scan log right. Sector quadrants give the space semantic geography.
Opportunities. Contacts on the scope. Radius = reachability, opacity = confidence, links = shared skills. Clusters are the unit of insight, not single roles.
Blocked. Dashed hollow contacts beyond the range ring, with the blocking term set in mono beneath — legible but visibly un-acquired.
Unlock. A sweep delta. Amber contacts ping in with an expanding ring, a new constellation edge draws itself, and the log writes the cause as entry 04.
Simulation vs real. Simulation is a numbered sweep; the log makes state history legible, so reverting is "go back to sweep 03" rather than an undo.
Agent. The log is the agent surface — enrichment and re-ranking appear as ordinary entries, plus an idle/next-sweep pill. Legible, never conversational.
Strengths. Most novel; the log doubles as an explainability and WebMCP story; clusters make the "just outside your orbit" thesis concrete.
Risks. Radar implies live data that may not exist; the sweep animation risks feeling decorative; three columns compete for attention.

DIRECTION C
The debugger is the product
Metaphor. A stack trace for a career. Each row is a condition; the orbit is the render target that redraws when a condition changes.
Layout. Editorial two-thirds ledger of constraints, one-third live orbit panel. Reading order is text-first, visual-second.
Opportunities. Mostly counts and named examples in the ledger; the orbit carries shape and proximity rather than labels, so numbers stay authoritative.
Blocked. A BLOCKS column with an honest count per constraint, plus LOCKED for things the user declared non-negotiable — blocking is attributed, not implied.
Unlock. An amber ledger strip: named roles, a count and the trade-off (median pay, time cost) — the most defensible framing of "what changed".
Simulation vs real. Per-row SIM badges and a single Commit/Reset pair; the orbit keeps the pre-edit ring as a dashed ghost.
Agent. A quiet "agent applied 2 edits" line in the header; agent-made rows would carry the same SIM badge, so its work is reviewable in place.
Strengths. Most trustworthy and legible; trade-offs are visible; easiest to demo verbally and cheapest to build well.
Risks. Closest to a conventional table UI — the wow moment depends entirely on the orbit panel's motion; risks reading as an admin tool at small sizes.
